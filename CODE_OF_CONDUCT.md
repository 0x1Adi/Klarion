# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.

## What we expect

Welcoming and respectful language. Openness to differing viewpoints and experience levels. Graceful acceptance of constructive feedback. Focus on what's best for the project and the people using it.

## What we won't accept

Harassment of any kind, public or private. Insults, personal attacks, or trolling. Sexualized language or imagery. Publishing someone's private information without permission. Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and any public space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers using GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/0x1Adi/Klarion/security/advisories/new), which works for conduct reports too and keeps them out of public view. Reports stay confidential.

Maintainers will review and respond as they judge appropriate, which can include a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban depending on severity. Maintainers who don't follow this in good faith may face the same consequences.
